How it works

Agents reserve with the message-bus tool (reserve, release, extend, resources) and start GPU jobs with agent-bus run <reservation> -- <command>, which makes only the reserved GPUs visible and marks the processes as the reservation's. Holds last at most 48 h and are booked at most 24 h ahead. A hold past its end is told, then expires 30 min later; one with nothing of its holder's running for 30 min is marked idle, and reclaimed after 2 h if someone is waiting. Nothing is enforced: a job started without a reservation shows up here as unclaimed use.
